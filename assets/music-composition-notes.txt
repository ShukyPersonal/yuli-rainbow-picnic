Rainbow Meadow

An original 48-second instrumental composed for Yuli's Rainbow Picnic.
The music was synthesized locally from mathematical oscillators. It contains
no external recordings, samples, or borrowed melodies.

16 bars at 80 beats per minute, in C major pentatonic. A rounded toy-piano
melody sits above quiet warm chords and occasional lower answering notes.
There is no percussion. Four small phrases include pauses and variations.
Short stereo reflections soften the sound; the loop has silent edge samples
and a gentle final fade, so its join cannot create a waveform click.

The game uses the AAC .m4a version at a conservative playback volume of 0.18.
The uncompressed stereo WAV is kept as a source asset, not an active download.
Music begins only through the game's play/control gestures, pauses with the
game or a hidden tab, and respects both global mute and the separate music
switch. Browser autoplay restrictions are handled without stopping the game.

Checks performed: signal peak/clipping/DC/loop-edge analysis, local AAC
encode/decode checks, and mocked playback-controller state/race checks.
An auditory listening review has not been performed in this environment.

Recreate: python3 work/compose-music.py
Encode: ffmpeg -i rainbow-meadow-theme.wav -c:a aac -b:a 96k rainbow-meadow-theme.m4a
